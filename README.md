Dashboard Overview
The dashboard includes interactive filters for device and model selection, brand highlights, and dynamic visualizations through pie charts and bar graphs.

Users can analyze sums of prices (in USD) across different brands and countries of origin.

Key Insights
Brand Analysis: Apple, Lenovo, HP, Samsung, Asus, and Dell are top contributors to the total price pool, each aggregating around 4K USD. Other major brands include Acer, Microsoft, Sony, and Google, with their price sums displayed in a descending order in the bar chart.

Pie Chart (Brand Distribution): The contributions of the primary brands are relatively evenly distributed, each accounting for approximately 8-13% of the total price value.

Country of Origin: The United States is the dominant country of origin, representing 48.19% (15K USD) of total product price value. Taiwan (19.24%), China (13%), South Korea & Japan (each 11.83%), and Malaysia (5.68%) follow, as seen in the pie chart.

Product Samples: Examples include Alienware (m15 R5), Aspire (5), Elite (Dragonfly), and Galaxy (S21 Ultra), highlighting diversity across laptops, smartphones, and elite models.
